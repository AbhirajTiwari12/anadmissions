03What we believe

The truth about top colleges.

No mysticism, no “holistic journey” seminar talk. Four things we actually believe, and build everything on.

I

A top school is not the meaning of life.

People build brilliant lives out of state schools every single year. If you hear nothing else from us, hear that first.

II

But the advantage is real. Brutally real.

And it isn’t the lectures. A Harvard lecture covers the same material as your community college. What you’re buying is the room: classmates who start companies, alumni who answer cold emails, the person down the hall who funds your startup in ten years. That network compounds for the rest of your life.

III

Admissions is a craft, not a lottery.

Every year, thousands of perfectly qualified students get rejected for avoidable reasons: flat essays, lazy positioning, a story that never comes together. Talent is rarely the difference. Craft is.

IV

Fresh intel beats faded credentials.

This game changes every cycle. We’re not consultants working from memories of the nineties. We sat where you’re sitting eighteen months ago, and you’ve already seen how that went.

04FAQ

Straight answers.

When should we start?

Whenever you’re ready. We work with students at every stage, from seventh graders choosing their first activities to seniors finishing their last supplemental. There’s something to sharpen at every point along the way.

How do sessions work?

It depends on what you’re after. Some students want structured coaching, where we work with them over time to build their extracurriculars, essays, and overall strategy. Others come to us as things come up: a question, a draft, a decision they’re stuck on. Most land somewhere in between. Either way, we work toward your goals, in the way that suits you.

Essay Review or Application Strategy?

Essay Review is asynchronous. You send a draft, and it comes back with in-line edits, comments, and a written report with clear next steps. Application Strategy is a live conversation about the bigger picture: activities, essays, timing, and where you’re applying. Plenty of students use both.

Who do you work with?

Anyone who wants the help. There’s no application to get in and no minimum GPA. If you’re interested in working with us, we’re in.

Why two students instead of a veteran consultant?

Because the game changed, and we just played it. Much of the industry still sells advice from admissions cycles that no longer exist. Every note we give comes from applications that worked last cycle, not folklore.
